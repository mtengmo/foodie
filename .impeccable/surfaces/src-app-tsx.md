# Foodie search, detail and recipes

Mode: Operate. Audience: everyday food lookup and home cooks. Scope: responsive bilingual app on static GitHub Pages, with local recipe calculation and persistence.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A useful food reference with a cookbook's warmth. Search, gram quantities and readable nutrients lead; no marketing hero obscures the working screen.

OWN-WORLD: Nordic Cookbook, selected on the decision board. Warm oat #faf7ef, deep green #283b31, berry #8f4059 and ochre #bb7a22; editorial headings, precise numerals, generous separation and compact native controls. Dark and alternate-accent themes inherit the same hierarchy.

STORY: Find a food in either language, inspect its edible-weight nutrients, then combine confirmed quantities into a locally saved recipe.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop top navigation, prominent search and results beside a spacious selected-food detail. Mobile search leads to a full detail route with Back. Quantity, energy, three macro values and a labelled chart are immediately discoverable. Chart/legend focus coordinates the selected macro without changing its meaning.

FORM: Nordic Cookbook, first grounded candidate, user-selected alternate; seed:115189b5f73101c4. Code-led build; chosen planning sketch .impeccable/mocks/decision/cookbook.html.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
